Privacy

Privacy Policy

Geography Gym is designed to work with local profiles but without accounts, passwords, advertising, or an application database.

Information stored on your device

Geography Gym stores local profile names, workout totals, answer statistics, streaks, flagged-question identifiers, unfinished-workout position, per-question answer history and scoring, theme, contrast, font-size and accent-color choices, sound and timer preferences, distance-unit choice, workout length, and tip preferences in your browser’s local storage. Each profile has separate data, and all profiles remain associated with that browser and device.

No Geography Gym account

The app does not require an account or password. A profile name is only a local label chosen by the person using the device; it is not transmitted by Geography Gym. The app does not ask for an email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.

Answer sounds

Correct and incorrect sounds are generated locally by the browser. The app does not record or transmit audio.

Offline storage

A service worker may cache app files on your device so Geography Gym can load faster and continue working offline. You can remove cached files and local progress by clearing site data in your browser.

Analytics and advertising

Geography Gym does not include advertising trackers or an application analytics service.

Hosting

The public app is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard technical information needed to deliver the website according to GitHub’s own policies.

Feedback

Send Feedback opens GitHub. Information you choose to submit there is shared with GitHub and may be public. Do not include personal or sensitive information in a public issue.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app adds features or changes how data is handled. The current version is dated September 15, 2026.
